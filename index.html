League Rules

Each manager has 3 college winners, 3 college losers, 3 NFL winners, and 3 NFL losers.

A winner pick earns 1 point for each team win. A loser pick earns 1 point for each team loss. Ties earn 0 points.

Postseason wins and losses count. College teams are unique across the league; NFL teams may appear up to 3 times.
